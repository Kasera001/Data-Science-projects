# F1 Grand Prix Prediction

An applied AI and data science project focused on estimating Formula 1 race outcomes through the analysis of performance, pace, and strategic indicators.

The project integrates:

- Recent driver and constructor performance
- Qualifying and race pace
- Circuit-specific characteristics
- Tire and pit-stop strategy
- Weather and track conditions
- Historical race data and contextual variables

The objective is to develop interpretable, data-driven predictions of race-winning probability while evaluating the factors that influence performance across different circuits and race scenarios.
